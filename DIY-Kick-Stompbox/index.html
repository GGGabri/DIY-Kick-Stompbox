Sample converter

DIY Kick Stompbox by 3Dvarius. Turns an audio file into a sound file for the pedal. Everything happens in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

1 · Choose a sound

Drop an audio file here, or click to choose one WAV works everywhere, MP3 / AIFF / FLAC / OGG depending on the browser. One hit, ideally under 0.7 s.

2 · Which sound is it?

Two sounds in the folder: the knob blends between them. Only one: the knob is its volume.

Advanced

Up to 680 ms each, sound A and sound B always fit together. Longer is fine with a single sound: the pedal has room for 1375 ms in total.

3 · Listen and save

Choose a sound first.

4 · Put it in the pedal

  1. Copy the saved file into the stompbox_kick folder, next to stompbox_kick.ino. Replace the old one if the computer asks.
  2. Open the .ino in the Arduino IDE and upload it again. If the sketch was already open, close it and open it again first, so the IDE sees the new file.
  3. In the Serial Monitor (115200 baud) the first lines tell you which sounds the pedal is using.
The sound is made the way the pedal plays it: mono, 16 kHz, 8 bit. Silence at the start is removed (it would delay the kick), the level is raised to full scale and the tail fades out to silence.
